Not a perfect solution, but is very modular
The idea is to keep a set of modules that will apply to every competition
These modules will form the base of the calculations, they will all be called from process.py and are straight drop-ins
Every competition will be unique and as such will require specific add-ons that can be added to process.py to complete the system
In the same vein report.py and inputs.py can also be modded accordingly to allow for competition specific vars and results
